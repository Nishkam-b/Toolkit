My story

From product shoots to AI

  1. Behind the camera

    I started out as a product photographer while I was still learning photography. My first ever job was an Amazon shoot — 20 bags, every one cut out by hand with Photoshop's pen tool onto a clean white background. It taught me patience, detail, and how much a good tool saves you.

  2. Falling for AI

    When AI tools arrived, I started testing them the way I used to test lenses — hands-on, one after another — and sharing the ones that genuinely saved time. People kept DMing questions, so my reels became the answers.

  3. Full-time creator

    Today I create full-time for 615K+ people, have worked with Google, Gemini and Meta AI, and was one of the early testers of Gemini and Muse in India.

  4. Now: building my own tools

    With zero coding background, I now build free tools with AI and show exactly how in my Vibe coded series — a PDF editor, a passport photo maker, a school ID card generator, and more on the way.
